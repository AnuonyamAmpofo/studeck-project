Studeck is a research project built as a University of Ghana Computer Science capstone. This policy explains what data the app collects during the pilot study, why, and who it's shared with.

What we collect

  • Account information — your email address and full name, either entered directly or provided by Google if you sign in with Google.
  • Courses you add — course name, code, and the learning type you assign it (calculation, concept, skill, or mixed).
  • Study session data — the study method(s) used, planned and actual duration, environment, whether focus mode was on, your self-rating of how the session went, and outcome scores from any practice problems you log.
  • Grades you choose to enter — assessment name and score, for the courses you add them to.
  • AI-generated quizzes — the topic or lecture material text you provide to generate a quiz, the generated questions, and your answers.

How it's used

Your session data is used to detect patterns specific to you — such as what time of day you tend to perform best, which study methods work for you, and how your self-assessed understanding compares to your actual quiz and problem scores. These insights are generated from your own data only. Studeck never combines one student's data with another's to generate insights, and does not compare you against other students.

Who we share data with

  • Supabase — our database host, where all app data is stored (PostgreSQL, hosted in the EU).
  • Google — only if you choose "Continue with Google" to sign in; Google provides your name and email to authenticate you.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — if you generate an AI quiz, the topic or lecture text you provide is sent to Anthropic to generate questions. Anthropic does not receive any other account data.

We do not sell or share your data with any other third party, and we do not use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can request that your account and all associated data be deleted at any time by contacting us at studeck-support@googlegroups.com. Participation in the Studeck pilot is voluntary, and you may stop using the app and request deletion at any point without giving a reason.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how your data is used can be sent to studeck-support@googlegroups.com.
